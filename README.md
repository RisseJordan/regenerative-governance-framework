## What this is

Compliance doesn't have to feel like drowning.

For most small and midsize organizations in regulated industries, AI governance lands as one more urgent, complex, underfunded thing on a list that's already too long. The regulations are real. The risk is real. And the resources to navigate both — written in plain language, sized for organizations that don't have a dedicated compliance team — are hard to find.

That's what this repository is.

A working library of practical governance resources. Plain language. Designed to be used, not filed. Built for the people inside regulated organizations who are doing their best with what they have — and who deserve tools that actually help them move forward with confidence.

## The Regenerative Governance Framework™

The belief underneath all of this work:

> Governance that isn't maintained isn't governance.

The Regenerative Governance Framework™ is built around four pillars. Not as a checklist — as a way of thinking about what it actually means to protect an organization and the people inside it.

**People** — Your staff are making AI decisions every day, often without guidance. Clear, accessible policy gives them permission to move forward confidently — and a structure to return to when they're unsure.

**Data** — Sensitive information doesn't stop being sensitive because it passed through an AI tool. This pillar is about knowing where your data goes and building controls that hold.

**Liability** — Documented, maintained governance isn't just protection — it's proof. When a regulator asks, or a client asks, or leadership asks, you have something real to show them.

**Capacity** — Good governance shouldn't add more weight to an organization that's already carrying a lot. It should hold — quietly, reliably — so the people inside can keep doing their work.
